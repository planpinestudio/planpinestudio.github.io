PlanPine Studio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

PlanPine Pin Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Etsy shops PlanPineStudio and TraceAndLearnCo to publish Pins about their own products to their own Pinterest account (pinterest.com/planpinestudio).

What data the app accesses. With the account owner's permission (Pinterest OAuth), the app reads the owner's boards and creates Pins on them. It does not access, collect or store data about any other Pinterest user.

What is stored. Only the OAuth access token for the owner's own account, kept on a private server and never shared. No personal data, analytics or tracking.

Sharing. No data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

Deletion. The owner can revoke access at any time in Pinterest settings (Settings → Security → Apps), after which the token stops working and is deleted.

Contact. Through the PlanPineStudio shop on Etsy: etsy.com/shop/PlanPineStudio
